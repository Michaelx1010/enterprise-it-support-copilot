# Multi-factor authentication recovery

## Lost phone or replaced authenticator

If the registered phone is lost or an authenticator did not transfer to a replacement phone, contact the service desk through the published support number. Complete the approved identity check before IT revokes the old registration and issues a time-limited enrollment session. Enroll the replacement device and test a fresh sign-in. Do not use a colleague's phone, share recovery codes, or request an MFA bypass.

## Codes rejected on the same phone

If the existing authenticator still opens but its one-time codes fail, enable automatic date and time on the phone and try one newly generated code. Confirm that the selected account belongs to Northstar. If codes still fail, record the application and error for Identity Support. Do not repeatedly submit codes, which may lock the account.

## Unrequested authentication prompts

Deny prompts you did not initiate and immediately contact Security through the published incident channel. Record the time and application shown. Do not approve a prompt to make notifications stop. Follow the phishing-reporting process if a suspicious message preceded the prompts. A working authenticator does not make unexpected prompts safe.
