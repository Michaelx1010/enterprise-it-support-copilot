# Software licenses and activation

## License allocation

Paid software requires an available company license and manager approval. Submit the product, business purpose, user, and requested duration to the software asset team. An application access group is separate from a license entitlement. Do not purchase a personal subscription or share a license key to work around an unavailable company seat.

## Activation fails after assignment

If a company license is already assigned but the installed client remains in trial mode, sign out and back in using the company account. Check that the installed edition matches the licensed edition and that public internet access works. Record the activation error and license-assignment confirmation for the software asset team. Do not paste license keys into tickets or change licensing-server settings.

## Transfer or reclaim a seat

When an employee changes roles or leaves, the application owner asks the software asset team to reclaim the seat. Transfers require confirmation that the previous allocation is no longer needed. Removing an application from a laptop does not necessarily release its subscription. Access revocation and license reclamation must both be confirmed.
