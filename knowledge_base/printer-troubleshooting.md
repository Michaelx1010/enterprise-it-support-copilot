# Office printer troubleshooting

## Printer missing from managed devices

Add the printer through the managed print catalog using the office and asset label. Do not install drivers from third-party websites. If the correct printer is absent from the catalog, send its asset label and office location to Endpoint Support. Guests cannot use the staff print queue; request a visitor printing arrangement through reception.

## Jobs stuck or printer offline

Check the physical printer for paper or toner messages and ask whether colleagues can print. Cancel only your own stuck jobs and retry one small document. If everybody is affected, send the asset label and display message to Endpoint Support. Do not restart the shared print server or clear other people's jobs. A printer repeatedly disappearing from the network requires Network Support investigation; do not assign a new IP address manually.

## Secure release does not recognize badge

Secure-print release uses employee badge enrollment, not the company password. Confirm the badge works for building access and note the printer asset label. Ask Facilities to verify badge enrollment and IT to check the print account link. Do not borrow another person's badge or leave confidential printouts unattended.
